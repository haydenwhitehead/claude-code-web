The Hunters

⚔

Dean Winchester

The Righteous Man

Eldest son of John and Mary Winchester. A fiercely loyal hunter with a love for classic rock, pie, and his 1967 Chevy Impala — "Baby." Dean carries the weight of the world on his shoulders but never loses his sense of humor.

  • Vessel of Michael
  • Bearer of the Mark of Cain
  • Wielder of the First Blade
📖

Sam Winchester

The Boy King

The younger Winchester brother. Intelligent, compassionate, and determined, Sam wanted a normal life but was pulled back into hunting. His psychic abilities and connection to the demon Azazel shaped his destiny.

  • Vessel of Lucifer
  • Stanford Pre-Law
  • Survivor of the Cage
✩

Castiel

Angel of the Lord

"I'm the one who gripped you tight and raised you from perdition." Castiel is a warrior of Heaven who chose humanity — and the Winchesters — over his angelic orders. Trenchcoat-wearing, pop-culture-confused, and fiercely devoted.

  • Seraph
  • Rebelled against Heaven
  • Team Free Will
☣

Crowley

King of Hell

The charming, sarcastic King of Hell. Once a Scottish tailor named Fergus MacLeod, Crowley rose through the demonic ranks with wit, cunning, and an appreciation for fine scotch. An unlikely ally to the Winchesters on many occasions.

  • King of the Crossroads
  • Master Dealmaker
  • Reluctant Hero
★

Bobby Singer

Surrogate Father

"Idjits!" Bobby Singer is the gruff but loving surrogate father to Sam and Dean. A master researcher and seasoned hunter, he runs a salvage yard in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, and is the go-to man for every hunter in the network.

  • Lore Expert
  • Hunter Hotline
  • Singer Salvage Yard
⚠

Jack Kline

The Nephilim

Son of Lucifer and Kelly Kline, Jack is a Nephilim of immense power. Despite his terrifying lineage, Jack chose goodness, guided by Castiel and the Winchesters. He sees the world with wonder and fights to protect it.

  • Son of Lucifer
  • Immense Cosmic Power
  • The New God

15 Seasons of the Road

1

Season 1 (2005)

The journey begins. Dean recruits Sam from Stanford to find their missing father. Together they face urban legends and discover the Yellow-Eyed Demon's plan.

2

Season 2 (2006)

John Winchester's sacrifice. Sam's psychic abilities grow. The brothers face the Roadhouse hunters, the Trickster, and the opening of the Devil's Gate.

3

Season 3 (2007)

Dean's crossroads deal ticks down. Ruby enters the picture. Lilith emerges as a new threat. The clock runs out and Dean is dragged to Hell.

4

Season 4 (2008)

Castiel raises Dean from perdition. Angels walk the Earth. Sam's demon blood addiction deepens. The seals break, and Lucifer rises.

5

Season 5 (2009)

The Apocalypse. Michael vs. Lucifer. Sam and Dean defy destiny and refuse to be vessels. Sam sacrifices himself to trap Lucifer in the Cage.

6-7

Seasons 6–7 (2010–2012)

Soulless Sam. Castiel's civil war in Heaven. The Leviathans are unleashed. Bobby makes his last stand. Purgatory awaits.

8-10

Seasons 8–10 (2012–2015)

The Trials to close Hell. The Mark of Cain. Demon Dean. Metatron's machinations. The Darkness is unleashed.

11-13

Seasons 11–13 (2015–2018)

Amara — God's sister. The British Men of Letters. Jack is born. Apocalypse World. Mary Winchester returns.

14-15

Seasons 14–15 (2018–2020)

Michael possesses Dean. Chuck revealed as the villain. The final battle for free will. "Carry On" — the last ride.

Hunter's Lore Guide

🔥 Salt & Iron

The basics of any hunter's toolkit. Salt repels and traps spirits; iron disrupts them. Never leave home without a salt-loaded shotgun.

☤ Devil's Trap

A pentagram symbol that traps demons within its boundaries. Painted on floors, carved into bullet casings, or tattooed on skin.

🔭 Angel Blade

A triangular silver blade wielded by angels. Capable of killing angels, demons, and most supernatural beings. Highly prized among hunters.

📘 The Colt

Samuel Colt's legendary revolver. "It can kill anything." Made in 1835 with only 13 original bullets. There are five things it cannot kill.

☠ The Mark of Cain

The original curse, passed from Lucifer to Cain, then to Dean. Grants immense power but corrupts the bearer. Its removal unleashed the Darkness.

📜 The Bunker

The Men of Letters bunker in Lebanon, Kansas. A fortress of supernatural knowledge, warded against all evil. Home base for the Winchesters.
